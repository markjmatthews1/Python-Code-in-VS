## ⚡ Quick Decision Matrix

### "Should I SELL today?"

**Check these 4 things:**

1. **Signal Color** = 🟢 READY TO SELL or 🔴 SELL NOW?
2. **Days Since Ex-Div** = 2-3 days (optimal window)?
3. **Backtest Says** = Is today the recommended day?
4. **Current Price** ≥ Target Sell Price?

**If YES to 3+ questions** → ✅ **SELL**  
**If NO to 2+ questions** → ⏸️ **WAIT**

## 📞 Emergency Scenarios

### Market Crash (>5% down):
- ⏸️ Pause rotations
- Hold current positions
- Wait for stabilization
- Review backtest confidence scores

### Ticker Suspended:
- Sell other positions normally
- Replace suspended ticker with next best from group
- Update rotation schedule

### Unexpected NAV Loss:
- Check backtest historical data
- Adjust expected recovery % in calculator
- Extend holding period if needed (max 5 days)
- Document for future reference

### Positive Surprise:
- Faster NAV recovery than expected
- Sell earlier if backtest confidence high
- Document for better future estimates
