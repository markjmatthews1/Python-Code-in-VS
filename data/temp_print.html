Perfect Strawberry Shortcake

📂 Dessert 🍽️ 8 servings ⏱️ Prep: 30 min plus cooling 🔥 Cook: 15 min

Ingredients

  • SHORTCAKE BISCUITS
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup plus 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) cold butter, cut up
  • 1 large egg, separated
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • SUGARED STRAWBERRIES
  • 2-1/2 pounds strawberries (8 cups)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • WHIPPED CREAM
  • 1 cup heavy or whipping cream
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspocn vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Prepare shortcakes: In bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and 1/3 cups sugar, Cut in butter (1).
  3. With fork, beat egg yolk with buttermilk: stir into flour mixture just until dough leaves side of bowl,
  4. On floured surface, with floured hands, knead dough 6 to 8 times to combine: pat to 3/4-inch thickness.
  5. Cut cut shortcakes (2); place 1 inch apart on ungreased large cookie sheet. Press trimmings together; cut to make 8 biscuits in all,
  6. With fork, beat egg whites, brush on short cakes. Sprinkle with sugar
  7. Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until golden. Coolon wire rack,
  8. Prepare Sugared Strawberries: Hull strawberries, then slice In large bowl, stir strawberries, sugar, and 1 tablespoon water, Let stand 15 minutes or refrigerate up to 4 hours.
  9. Prepare Whipped Cream: In medium bowl, beat cream, sugar. and vanilla until stiff peaks form,
  10. Split each shorteake. Place botcom halves on 8 plates, Layer berries and cream over shortcake bortoms; replace tops.
